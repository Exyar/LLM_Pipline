How Language Models Understand Words

Neural networks are mathematical functions that cannot read letters directly. Instead, raw text undergoes a 4-step transformation: Tokenization into discrete integer IDs, Vocabulary Counting, Dense 3D Vector Embedding where semantic concepts become spatial coordinates, and Cosine Similarity calculations.

Why Tokens & Vocabularies?

Computers operate on binary numbers, not letters. The tokenizer splits human text into discrete integer IDs (0 to |V|-1). The vocabulary table registers every recognized token and its statistical frequency.

2

Why Continuous 3D Vectors?

One-hot IDs have no concept of similarity (dog and puppy are completely orthogonal). Embeddings give each word coordinates in multi-dimensional space, capturing rich semantic relationships and analogies geometrically.

3

How Similarity Powers Attention

In Transformer architectures, the Attention mechanism computes the dot product between word vectors. Words that share high cosine similarity naturally route attention to each other during generation!
